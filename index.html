How I work

Three principles I keep coming back to.

01

Start with the user.

Technology matters only when it solves something people actually care about.

02

Understand the system.

Good product decisions come from understanding technical constraints, incentives, workflows, and edge cases.

03

Build, test, iterate.

I prefer prototypes, experiments, and real user feedback over endless speculation.
